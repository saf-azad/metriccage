This site used to assert that the model had excellent calibration and mediocre discrimination, and then show neither the forecast distribution nor a baseline. Every chart below exists because that sentence deserved evidence. Two of them ended up contradicting things published elsewhere on this site.

01 · the central claim, as arithmetic

Calibration and discrimination are two terms of one equation.

Murphy’s decomposition splits the Brier score into exactly the two quantities this project argues about, plus the part nobody can beat. Stating them separately turns an assertion into an identity.

02 · value added

A four-model ensemble is worth exactly what it beats.

The first question any reviewer asks about an ensemble is whether it outperforms its own best component. The second is what each component is actually contributing. The site used to answer neither, and showed the fitted weights as if they were a result.

03 · what actually drives it

Two ways to rank a feature. They disagree completely.

XGBoost’s built-in gain is biased toward continuous and high-cardinality splits and is unstable across refits. It was also being read off one stream carrying a third of the ensemble weight, to support a claim about the whole system.

04 · the limitation, measured

A fighter’s record begins the day they enter the UFC.

This is the project’s top stated structural limitation, and it used to be supported by one anecdote about a card in Macau. An anecdote is not a limitation; it is a story about one. Here is the same claim as a measurement, and here is what the anecdote is actually worth once you ask the right question of it.

05 · stability

In-sample years, marked as in-sample.

Reproduction. Every figure on this page comes from re-scoring the shipped four-stream bundle on the frozen temporal split — train 2005–2022, validate 2023, test 2024-01-01 to 2025-05-04, giving 5,563 / 504 / 695 fights. The re-score reproduces the bundle’s own recorded test metrics to within 1×10−16, so nothing here is an approximation of the deployed model — it is the deployed model. Scripts: analysis/model_eval.py, analysis/model_eval2.py.

06 · named, not hidden

What this page still cannot show you.

The closing line. The pipeline diagram lists BestFightOdds as a source and the odds scraper is a documented stub, so no market corpus exists over the test window. Model log-loss against the closing line on the same 695 fights is the single most informative chart this page could carry, and it is the one it does not have. Losing to the market would still be a strong result if the margin were shown.
Method of victory. The two-stage head reports AUC around 0.56 in prose and appears in no chart. A three-class reliability plot or a confusion matrix would be more convincing than the confession, because it would show the thing was measured.
Pre-UFC records. Named as the top structural limitation and now quantified (−0.114 AUC on near-debutant matchups), but not yet fixed. Tapology and Sherdog are already in the collection step; even a crude count of prior professional fights would turn the blind spot into a confidence modifier.
A better calibrator. Isotonic on 504 validation fights emits 24 distinct values. Beta calibration, or isotonic refit on a larger validation window, would keep the calibration and return the resolution the step function is currently throwing away. Untested here, so unclaimed here.
