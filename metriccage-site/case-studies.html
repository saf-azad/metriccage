Method

How these were scored.

Each card was predicted before it took place, using only data available at that moment — no post-hoc feature updates, no re-running with the benefit of hindsight. A pick counts as correct when the fighter given the higher win probability wins.

Card-level records are reported as raw counts rather than percentages, because a thirteen-fight card is far too small a sample to express as a rate without implying a precision that is not there. Nine out of thirteen is nine out of thirteen; it is not "69% accuracy," and treating it as such is exactly the kind of overclaiming this project is built to avoid.

The aggregate metrics on the results page — computed across 695 fights — are the numbers that carry statistical weight. These cards are here for what they reveal about failure modes, not as evidence of performance.
