Model evidence

Every chart on this page is computed from the shipped four-stream bundle, re-scored on the frozen temporal split. Each one names the file it came from, so any figure here can be traced back to the script that produced it.

01

Brier decomposition

Murphy’s decomposition splits the Brier score into the two quantities this project cares about, plus the part no model can beat. Stating them separately turns a claim about calibration into an identity.

Brier 0.2286 = reliability 0.0033 − resolution 0.0240 + uncertainty 0.2496 · Brier skill over the base rate +0.084

The decomposition, to scale

Reliability at 0.0033 is the calibration term, and near zero is where you want it. Uncertainty at 0.2496 is the irreducible difficulty of the sport itself.

Forecast distribution

The isotonic calibrator maps 695 blended probabilities onto 24 distinct values. The largest, 0.5621, covers 207 fights.

28% of forecasts sit outside the 30–70 band

Isotonic is the calibrator that earns its place: test log-loss 0.6463, against 0.6498 for Platt scaling and 0.6512 uncalibrated.

Baselines and ablation

XGBoost carries the most, at +0.0072 log-loss when removed, followed by logistic regression and the LSTM. Elo sits near neutral because its signal already reaches the blend through the other three streams as features.

03

Feature importance

Permutation on held-out data is the measure used here. Shuffle one feature on fights the model has never seen, and record how much worse the forecast gets — a test that no feature passes on the strength of how it looked during training.

Permutation importance — Δ log-loss when a feature is shuffled

Measured on the 695-fight blind test set. Higher is more important.

analysis/model_eval2.json · permutation importance

Reading the ranking

Age difference leads at +0.049, pace mismatch follows at +0.038, and the two fighters’ UFC fight counts take third and fourth. Freshness, tempo and depth of record — in that order.

Permutation is the right test here because it is computed on data the model has never seen. A tree model’s built-in gain is measured on the training data and rewards features that can be split many ways, which makes it a description of how the trees were built rather than of what predicts a fight.

Both experience counts reaching the top four is the reason performance scales with fighter history, measured directly in section 04.

04

Performance by fighter experience

The model reads a fighter through their UFC record, so the amount of record available is worth measuring directly rather than describing. Split the same blind test set by how much history each matchup carries and the effect is clear.

AUC by fighter experience

Same model, same test window — split by how much UFC history is available for the matchup.

analysis/model_eval2.json · experience_auc

What this establishes

On matchups where both fighters have five or more UFC bouts, the model reaches AUC 0.718 — comfortably its strongest regime, and the one that covers most of the card on a typical event.

The measurement also sets the priority for what comes next. Extending fighter history with pre-UFC records is the leading roadmap item precisely because this chart puts a number on what it is worth, rather than leaving it as an assumption.

Tapology and Sherdog are already in the collection step. The gap of 0.114 is the size of the opportunity.

06

Reproduction

Everything above comes from re-scoring the shipped four-stream bundle on the frozen temporal split, so the charts describe the deployed model rather than a rebuild of it.

The split

Train 2005–2022, validate 2023, test 2024-01-01 to 2025-05-04 — giving 5,563 / 504 / 695 fights. The boundaries are fixed dates and do not move between runs.

The check

The re-score reproduces the bundle’s recorded test metrics to 2.2×10⁻¹⁶ — machine precision. Nothing on this page is an approximation of the deployed model.

The scripts

analysis/model_eval.py, analysis/model_eval2.py and analysis/score_streams.py emit the JSON these charts read. Re-running them against a newer data snapshot regenerates the figures.
